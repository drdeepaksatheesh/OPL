## Student inquiry workflow

1. Identify the organ/preparation and source, including animal species and whether it is an isolated or intact model.
2. Read the recording chain: stimulus, electrode/transducer, gain, acquisition, units, sample period.
3. Inspect the unchanged original waveform, image or sample file.
4. Check polarity and calibration, independently set measurement cursor(s); do not erase the recorded baseline.
5. Compute only quantities justified by available scale information.
6. Compare normal, altered and artifact conditions; identify ambiguity explicitly.
7. Document observations and cite source ID + original dataset/paper, not just the OPL viewer.
