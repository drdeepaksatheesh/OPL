# Contributing

Propose a source first: give a stable DOI/URL, title, species, preparation, original waveform location, licence evidence and (where known) signal calibration. A published image alone is welcome as a **FIGURE** or **SCAN**, not RAW data. Avoid uncredited screenshots. Third-party data submissions must contain confirmed permission to redistribute.

For an accepted dataset, retain original bytes unchanged, compute SHA256, write a versioned sidecar manifest, and document transformations. Contributors are encouraged to report wrong interpretations and bad calibrations; corrections should be documented, not silently overwritten.

Never upload identifiable patient recordings, unapproved animal data, third-party manuals under restrictive terms, or photos containing student identity. No need to perform an animal experiment to contribute: historical scans, digital datasets and teaching peer review are sufficient.

**Scientific review checklist:** preparation, polarity/direction, axis units, timestamps, intervention, physiological interpretation, artifacts, alternate explanations, licence and attribution.
