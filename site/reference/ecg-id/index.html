What this first lab does — and does not claim

It preserves the source record, distinguishes raw from source-provided filtered data, exposes the baseline used for vertical measurements, and calculates time directly from the source sampling rate. It is an educational/research visualization tool, not a diagnostic ECG device.

The ECG-ID beat annotations are automated and unaudited; they are shown as source metadata, not treated as cardiologist gold-standard annotations.
