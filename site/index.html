OPENPHYSIOLOGYLAB

Record. Analyse. Understand.

A growing collection of small, transparent physiology instruments for teaching, low-cost acquisition and reproducible biomedical experimentation.

Public-site prototype · individual modules are released only after they pass the OPL release gate.

Use the instrument, not the repository

Each OPL module is designed to work as a focused tool on its own and later connect to the larger OpenPhysiologyLab workflow. Source, validation notes and citation information accompany released versions.
